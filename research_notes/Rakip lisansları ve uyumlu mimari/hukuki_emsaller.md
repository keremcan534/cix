# Legal precedents: when chance-won virtual currency/items are a gambling "prize", and when host/streamer gift income can be separated from it

Research notes, current as of 25 November 2026 (all sources accessed 25 Nov 2026). Research, not legal advice.
Labels: **VERIFIED** = primary text opened and read in this session; **SECONDARY** = law-firm/press/court-news report, primary not opened; **UNVERIFIED** = from memory or a snippet only, not confirmed. Detailed TCK 228 / 7258 analysis is left to another researcher.

App context used for "relevance" (from `docs/LUCKY_HEDIYE.md`): the sender of a Şanslı/Uğurlu hediye pays coins, the server draws a multiplier (2x–1000x, default expected return ~x0.65) and credits **coins** back to the sender; the recipient (host) gets a **fixed 10% of the gift value as diamonds whatever the draw** (`lucky_config.sharePct`). Coins (bought or won) can be gifted to hosts, whose diamonds are cashed out (para çekme, Jeton Noktası, agency payouts). Other chance features with purchased coins: Oyun Salonu, Rüzgâr Gülü, Oda roketi.

## Q1. Precedent table: what decided "money's worth / thing of value"

### Takeaway
Across the UK, EU and US civil cases the deciding factor is almost always **whether the chance output can reach real money**: by the operator, by a mechanism inside the app (user-to-user transfer), or by a secondary market that the operator tolerates or helps. A fully closed loop usually escapes gambling law (UKGC 2017, NL 2022, AT OGH 2025, Mason, Coffee/Taylor at district level), with Washington State as the strict exception ("extending play" alone is value: Kater). China, the closest analogue to a voice-room app, has a long line of **criminal convictions of voice-chat / live-streaming platforms** where lucky-draw gifts were sent to hosts and cashed out through host withdrawals or host/guild "buyback" (收渣/回购), plus a 2023 enforcement wave that shut 20+ voice-social apps.

### Cited Findings (key verbatim points)
- UKGC: "Where in-game items or currencies which can be won, traded or sold can be converted into cash or exchanged for items of value, under gambling legislation they are considered money or money's worth." — [UKGC 2017 paper, p.1](https://assets.ctfassets.net/j16ev64qyf6l/4A644HIpG1g2ymq11HdPOT/ca6272c45f1b2874d09eabe39515a527/Virtual-currencies-eSports-and-social-casino-gaming.pdf)
- UKGC: "Where prizes are successfully restricted for use solely within the game, such in-game features would not be licensable gambling, notwithstanding the elements of expenditure and chance." (§3.18) — [UKGC 2017](https://assets.ctfassets.net/j16ev64qyf6l/4A644HIpG1g2ymq11HdPOT/ca6272c45f1b2874d09eabe39515a527/Virtual-currencies-eSports-and-social-casino-gaming.pdf)
- UKGC: in the casino-chip analogy one entity controls activity, gambling and cash-out, but "it is possible for these three functions to be offered by three different parties" (§3.15); enforcement priority turns on "the proximity of any facilities for gambling to the means of exchanging items for cash, overt relationships between the two and/or the ease with which such transactions are conducted" (§3.20). — [UKGC 2017](https://assets.ctfassets.net/j16ev64qyf6l/4A644HIpG1g2ymq11HdPOT/ca6272c45f1b2874d09eabe39515a527/Virtual-currencies-eSports-and-social-casino-gaming.pdf)
- UKGC on loyalty points: points solely from actions unrelated to outcome (e.g. friend referrals) are "unlikely to present an issue"; points earned by time played are "related to the outcomes of the game of chance (the more chips they win, the longer they can play)"; if they can be traded for goods/services with monetary value "we would consider this would make the game a licensable activity" (§5.16); operators must ensure "loyalty schemes offering articles or services which have a monetary value are not linked to the outcome of games of chance" (§5 summary). — [UKGC 2017](https://assets.ctfassets.net/j16ev64qyf6l/4A644HIpG1g2ymq11HdPOT/ca6272c45f1b2874d09eabe39515a527/Virtual-currencies-eSports-and-social-casino-gaming.pdf)
- Kater: chips "extend the privilege of playing"; "if a user wins chips, the user wins the privilege of playing Big Fish Casino without charge"; nothing in Bullseye conditioned value "on a user's ability to redeem those points for money or merchandise"; but a ToS-prohibited black market cannot found value (fn 2, citing Mason, 851 F.3d at 320 n.3). — [9th Cir. opinion](https://cdn.ca9.uscourts.gov/datastore/opinions/2018/03/28/16-35010.pdf)
- Mason: "Machine Zone retained the money that Mason paid to obtain virtual gold regardless of the outcome"; "What she could not do is cash out of the game." — [FindLaw](https://caselaw.findlaw.com/us-4th-circuit/1853092.html) (via search snippet)
- AT OGH 6 Ob 228/24h: packs "must not be assessed in isolation, but only within the overall context of the game"; unofficial trading outside the contractual environment "cannot influence the gambling-law assessment". — [DLA Piper](https://www.dlapiper.com/insights/publications/2026/01/supreme-court-shifts-the-playing-field-lootboxes-fall-outside-the-austrian-gambling-act)
- 星螺 judgment: "张某某以营利为目的，建立语聊平台网站并利用赌博插件接受投注，组织赌博活动，情节严重"; ordinary gift + commission model "属正常经营模式"; problem: "将提现对象扩大到主播之外的不特定人". — [Beijing News](https://www.bjnews.com.cn/detail/1740961798168119.html)
- Changsha: "以上对赌且直接折现的行为属于赌博类违法犯罪行为". — [Guangming](https://m.gmw.cn/2025-04/07/content_1304009891.htm)
- Lawyer compilation: platform that "提供技术接口、分成抽佣并默许主播回购，属于共同开设赌场"; even if buyback is done by a host or third party, "只要平台知情或默许，仍会被视为赌场闭环的组织者"; mere "随机性+输赢结果" or "充值＋打赏主播" is not per se illegal. — [Sohu](https://www.sohu.com/a/917027240_120717401)
- 2023 wave: lawyer 麻策: design combining "投入产出有法币", "随机概率玩法" and "以小博大" is the core element of gambling-type play; Banban still downloadable from Apple App Store while police had taken its team. — [Toutiao/21st Century](https://www.toutiao.com/zixun/7530808225760036883)

### Inferences
- The decisive question for our app is not the odds table but **whether chance-won coins can reach a host's diamonds and from there money**. If they can, every Western "closed loop" defence (UKGC §3.18, DCMS 2022, Mason, Coffee/Taylor, NL, AT) falls away, and the pattern matches the Chinese voice-room convictions (#20–#23) almost exactly: purchased coins → chance → coins → gift to host → host withdrawal (+ possible off-platform refund to the player).
- The Western loot-box wins (NL, AT) relied on the chance element being embedded in a skill game; stand-alone wheels, lucky gifts and rockets cannot use that defence.
- Kater shows that in some jurisdictions (Washington; similar "extend play" language exists in other US states, not checked here) even a perfectly closed coin loop is gambling.

## Q2. Can host gift income be legally separated from chance outcomes? Any decisions applying such a separation?

### Takeaway
No court or regulator decision was found that expressly approves "host gets a fixed share of the purchase price regardless of the random result" as making a chance feature lawful. What the sources do support: (a) operator/host revenue that is fixed regardless of outcome helped in Mason, but only together with "cannot cash out"; (b) UKGC §5.16 says any monetary-value stream that grows with chance winnings is "linked to the outcome"; (c) Chinese courts treat ordinary gift tipping with a revenue share as a normal business, and convict where chance-won gifts are routed to hosts who cash out or buy back. So the separation is only credible if **chance-derived value can never be gifted, transferred or converted**, not just if the host's percentage is fixed.

### Cited Findings
- Fixed-revenue argument appears in Mason ("retained the money ... regardless of the outcome"), but the court paired it with "she could not ... cash out". — [FindLaw](https://caselaw.findlaw.com/us-4th-circuit/1853092.html)
- UKGC: loyalty points that grow with chips won are "related to the outcomes of the game of chance"; tradeable for monetary-value goods → licensable (§5.16). By analogy, diamonds that grow when chance-won coins are gifted are linked to chance. — [UKGC 2017](https://assets.ctfassets.net/j16ev64qyf6l/4A644HIpG1g2ymq11HdPOT/ca6272c45f1b2874d09eabe39515a527/Virtual-currencies-eSports-and-social-casino-gaming.pdf)
- China: gift tipping with a commission is "正常经营模式"; 星螺 crossed the line because won gifts were sent to hosts and cashed out, with ~80% returned to players privately. — [Beijing News](https://www.bjnews.com.cn/detail/1740961798168119.html)
- China: lawyers' red lines: forbid user-to-user transfer of virtual assets; block gift/points → cash channels and keep consumption one-way; do not tie the platform's take to win/loss; watch for money flowing back via third-party payment, guilds or host payouts; the platform's prior ban on private buyback and prompt enforcement decide whether it "knew or tolerated". — [Sohu](https://www.sohu.com/a/917027240_120717401)
- China: host income on a platform found to be a gambling house may be frozen or treated as illicit proceeds. — [Toutiao/21st Century](https://www.toutiao.com/zixun/7530808225760036883)
- UK: industry must not be "passive" to third-party cash-out; DCMS 2025 review recommends developers not build APIs enabling external gambling and act on misuse. — [UKGC 2017 §3.16](https://assets.ctfassets.net/j16ev64qyf6l/4A644HIpG1g2ymq11HdPOT/ca6272c45f1b2874d09eabe39515a527/Virtual-currencies-eSports-and-social-casino-gaming.pdf); [CMS](https://cms.law/en/gbr/legal-updates/inside-the-skins-gambling-surge-dcms-review-exposes-risks-and-regulatory-gaps-in-skins-gambling)
- Streamer income from promoting gambling is policed separately: FutGalaxy (UK conviction incl. a YouTuber's advertising), FTC CSGOLotto (endorsement order), Twitch 2022 ban on unlicensed slots/roulette/dice streams. — [Pinsent Masons](https://www.pinsentmasons.com/out-law/news/gambling-regulator-secures-landmark-prosecutions-in-e-sports-betting-case); [FTC](https://www.ftc.gov/enforcement/cases-proceedings/162-3184/csgolotto-trevor-martin-thomas-cassell); [NPR](https://www.npr.org/2022/09/22/1124384092/twitch-bans-some-gambling-content-after-an-outcry-from-streamers)

### Inferences (design factors, synthesized)
Factors that move a design **towards gambling**:
1. Chance output can be converted to money by the operator (direct cash-out) — all jurisdictions.
2. Chance output can reach money **through another user** (gift to a host who withdraws; user-to-user transfer; host/guild buyback) — UKGC §3.15/§3.20, Kater facts, Chinese cases #20–#22.
3. Operator tolerates or facilitates a secondary market/cash-out (fees on transfers in Kater; "tacit" buyback in China; API in DCMS 2025).
4. Paid entry with purchased currency (UKGC "expenditure"; China 2016 ban on fiat/virtual-currency entry; Belgium).
5. Stand-alone chance feature, not embedded in a skill game (contrast NL 2022, AT 2025).
6. Win-scaled rewards with monetary value (UKGC §5.16 loyalty points).
7. "Small for big" multipliers and win broadcasts that induce play (Chinese 以小博大; UKGC §3.11 on misleading winning streaks).
8. In "extend play" jurisdictions (Washington), winning more play alone is value (Kater).
Factors that move it **away**:
1. Chance output usable only in-app, non-transferable, non-giftable, non-convertible, with ToS ban and active enforcement (UKGC §3.18; Kater fn 2; Mason; Coffee; AT OGH).
2. Operator/host revenue fixed regardless of outcome (Mason), **only** in combination with no cash-out path.
3. Host income only from gifts bought with purchased coins at a fixed price, with chance-won coins in a separate, non-giftable balance (inference; no decision found applying it).
4. Truthful published odds and logged changes (Korea Nexon; China 2016; store rules) — necessary but not sufficient.
5. Monitoring/blocking off-platform refunds between hosts and senders (China "know or tolerate" test).
Specific to our app: the fixed 10% host share in `lucky_config.sharePct` addresses only the first leg; won coins credited to the sender's normal balance can be gifted to hosts and withdrawn, which is the loop in #20–#23.

### Gaps
- No decision found explicitly applying a "fixed host share, separate non-giftable chance balance" separation; it remains an argument by analogy.
- TikTok LIVE / Bigo / other voice-room gift policies on chance gifts not checked.
- No Turkish authority found on host gift income vs gambling prize.

## Q3. App-store presence is not evidence of legality

### Takeaway
Several long-running, store-listed products were later penalised or sued: Chinese voice-social apps were shut by police in 2023 (Banban still on Apple's App Store at the time), Big Fish Casino was found illegal in Washington after years on the stores, Nexon was fined in 2024 for odds conduct going back to 2010, and Apple, Google and Meta themselves are defendants in the social-casino MDL.

### Cited Findings
- Banban and Huanhuan were still downloadable from Apple's App Store while police had taken Banban's team and frozen >2bn yuan; 20+ voice platforms shut, suspended or removed from May 2023. — [Toutiao/21st Century](https://www.toutiao.com/zixun/7530808225760036883)
- Big Fish Casino (free download, in-app chip purchases) held illegal gambling under Washington law in 2018 after the plaintiff had played since 2013. — [Kater opinion](https://cdn.ca9.uscourts.gov/datastore/opinions/2018/03/28/16-35010.pdf)
- KFTC fined Nexon 11.642bn won in Jan 2024 for odds changes from 2010–2021. — [Asia Economy](https://www.asiae.co.kr/en/article/2024010311500851221)
- App stores sued in the simulated casino-style games MDL; 9th Cir. declined early §230 ruling (2024); district court refused §230 dismissal (Oct 2025). — [Law360](https://law360.com/articles/1839563)
- 星螺约玩 had operated under an earlier name (声甜语音) before the 2024 convictions. — [Beijing News](https://www.bjnews.com.cn/detail/1740961798168119.html)

### Inferences
- Store review checks odds disclosure and age rating, not gambling law in each country; liability later can reach the operator, its staff (星螺 employees convicted of 帮信罪) and hosts (Changsha host 3y6m), and host balances can be frozen.

### Gaps
- Whether Apple/Google removed any Turkish voice-room app for chance gifts was not checked.
