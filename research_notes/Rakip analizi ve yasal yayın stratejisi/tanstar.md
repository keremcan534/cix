# TanStar (iOS id6746351641): competitor dossier

All sources accessed 2026-11-25 unless noted. Public pages only. No account, no install, no APK or IPA download.

**Headline finding:** TanStar is **not** listed as a voice-room / live social app like Similar Club. Its App Store listing presents it as an **"AI character chat" app**: chat rooms with AI characters, voice calls with AI characters, pay per message with coins, and virtual gifts to AI characters. Turkish reviews, however, describe paying coins to message "someone", constant incoming messages meant to sell coins, "bot" profiles asking for money, and blackmail with users' videos by "people inside" the app. They also say an Android version exists with more features (follow, friends, family groups). I could not find that Android version on Google Play. Gambling-type games: no evidence on any public page.

## 4. Money flow (top-up → coins → gifts/games → earners → cash)

### Takeaway
On public evidence the flow is **Apple IAP → coin packages → per-message fees, AI voice calls and virtual gifts**. The store and the documents show nothing about earners, diamonds or cash-out. Reviews suggest real people may be on the other side ("people inside", "bot profiles asking for money"), but that is not verified.

### Relevance for Similar Club (brief)
- On public evidence TanStar is an adjacent competitor (paid AI/companion chat), not a direct voice-room clone. Its Turkish reviews show the risks of pay-to-message flows: complaints of "para tuzağı" and blackmail. Similar Club's moderation, contact filter and anti-extortion measures should be defended against the same patterns.
