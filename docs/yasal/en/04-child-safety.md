## 1. Age and scope
SimilarClub is designed for adults aged 18 or over. Risk-proportionate age assurance and applicable store tools will be used to prevent child access. Self-declaration alone is not assumed sufficient; complete [AGE ASSURANCE METHOD AND RISK ASSESSMENT].
## 2. Zero tolerance
Child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), child sexual abuse material (CSAM), grooming, sexual solicitation, sextortion, sexual requests directed at children, child trafficking and content facilitating or encouraging these activities are prohibited. This includes real, generated and altered abusive content.
## 3. Safe reporting
Report suspected accounts, rooms, message times and incident descriptions through the in-app reporting tool or support@similarclub.online. Do not copy or transmit illegal imagery. For imminent danger contact local law enforcement or emergency services. Child safety reports will receive priority.
## 4. Response and authorities
Restrict access to suspected content and take appropriate account action; preserve evidence only as legally necessary with authorised access. Confirmed CSAM is reported to NCMEC or the relevant regional authority as required by applicable reporting obligations. Users are not asked to redistribute material. Define [REPORTING AUTHORITIES, PROCEDURE AND RESPONSIBLE TEAM].
## 5. Contact and oversight
Proposed child safety contact: support@similarclub.online. Verify that the designated Google Play contact can access and respond through this channel before launch. Provide a safe appeal route for age-related mistakes and safety decisions. Training, incident records, risk review and periodic checks will be implemented. Adult-only registration does not remove child safety obligations.
