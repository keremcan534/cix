## 1. Request channels
You can initiate permanent SimilarClub account deletion inside and outside the app. Proposed app path: Settings > Account > Delete my account. Web path: https://similarclub.online/delete-account. Both must be functional before publication. Alternative help: support@similarclub.online. Uninstalling the app does not delete the account.
## 2. Process
The requester undergoes proportionate security verification. Explain deletion consequences, active subscriptions, purchased coins and confirmed host/agency earnings. Do not introduce unnecessary phone or support barriers. Once confirmed, account access ends and the user receives request status and completion information.
## 3. Data scope
Accounts, profiles, uploaded media, associated user content and other linked personal data are erased or, where appropriate, irreversibly anonymised. Other users' rights, independent records and legally required data are assessed separately. Identity, payment, tax, fraud and security records may be retained only for documented valid reasons with restricted access. Complete [RETENTION BY DATA TYPE, BACKUP DELETION SCHEDULE AND PROVIDER DELETION PROCESS]. Freezing an account alone is not deletion.
## 4. Subscriptions and accrued amounts
An active store subscription may require cancellation through the relevant store; provide guidance within the deletion flow. Open payouts, refunds and lawful confirmed claims are resolved through a separate channel. Deletion must not unnecessarily delay mandatory privacy rights.
## 5. Timing and rights
Requests are answered within applicable legal deadlines. Under UK GDPR generally within one month, and under KVKK where applicable no later than 30 days; explain lawful extensions and exceptions. If Sign in with Apple is used, revoke relevant tokens. Send necessary deletion requests to service providers. Contact support for complaints and information.
