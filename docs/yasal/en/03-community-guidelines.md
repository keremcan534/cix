## 1. Safe community
These rules cover profiles, room names, chats, audio/video communication, gifts, games and all agency and host conduct. Participation is voluntary; refusal and blocking decisions must be respected.
## 2. Prohibited conduct
Harassment, threats, stalking, hate speech, bullying, doxxing, blackmail, non-consensual intimate imagery, pornography, sexual services or compensated romantic encounters, trafficking, child abuse and illegal activity are prohibited. Do not record or share without appropriate permission. Impersonation, account trading, spam, fraud, cheating, fake gift transactions and misleading earnings claims are prohibited. Users must not be pressured to pay for chats; automated messages must not masquerade as personally written messages.
## 3. Reporting and blocking
Users may report content or accounts through the in-app Report function and stop contact with Block. Confirm [ACTUAL SCREEN PATH]. Contact support@similarclub.online. For immediate danger contact local emergency services. Do not download, reproduce or email child abuse material; report account and incident details instead.
## 4. Moderation and enforcement
Content filtering, report review and proportionate measures are applied; verify scope and response targets in [MODERATION PLAN]. Severity, repetition and safety risk are considered. Measures include warnings, removal, communication restrictions, temporary suspension or permanent termination. Serious cases are promptly escalated to safety staff and competent authorities where required.
## 5. Appeals
Users may submit the decision date, account identifier and reasons to support@similarclub.online. Reasons and outcomes are communicated where compatible with safety. Appeals are reviewed separately from the initial decision where practicable. Legal remedies remain available.
